\textbf{Re: Submission of the manuscript ``Robust multi-objective optimization of roundwood timber bridges: Structural performance and parametric design relationships''}

\bigskip
Dear Editor,

\bigskip
We are pleased to submit the manuscript entitled \textit{``Robust multi-objective optimization of roundwood timber bridges: Structural performance and parametric design relationships''} for consideration for publication in \textit{Structures}.

The manuscript presents a parametric, robust multi-objective optimization framework for roundwood timber bridges, coupling NSGA-II to a structural model of the girder and deck and accounting for the natural dimensional variability of roundwood logs directly in the search. Applied to 20 configurations covering four spans and five strength classes, the framework reveals how span, strength class and dimensional variability control material efficiency and the governing limit states.

The main findings show that bending governs all 20 configurations, with the deck controlling 15 of them, while girder deflection already approaches its limit in the most severe cases. The first strength class upgrade (D20 to D30) delivers 41 to 48\% of all the timber saving available up to D60, and robustness carries a real, monotonic cost of +6.0 to +26.9\% in timber volume. These results are condensed into design charts and a closed-form pre-sizing equation ($R^2 = 0.999$) for direct use in preliminary design within the investigated domain.

To the best of our knowledge, this is the first study to integrate parametric modelling, multi-objective optimization and robustness analysis for roundwood timber bridges, a structural system still underexplored relative to concrete and steel bridges. We believe its combination of methodological novelty, structural insight and practical design tools fits well within the scope of \textit{Structures}.

This manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved its submission and declare no conflict of interest.

We appreciate your consideration and look forward to your response.
