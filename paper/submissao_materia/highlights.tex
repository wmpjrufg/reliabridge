\noindent\textbf{Robust multi-objective optimization of roundwood timber bridges: Structural performance and parametric design relationships}

\bigskip
\begin{itemize}
    \item Parametric robust multi-objective optimization framework for timber bridges.
    \item Robustness uses a deterministic diameter-perturbation grid, not random sampling.
    \item Timber consumption ranges from 1.75 to 9.98 m\textsuperscript{3} across 20 cases.
    \item Bending governs all 20 cells; deck bending controls 15 of them.
    \item Robustness cost is real: up to +26.9\% extra volume at 20\% deviation.
\end{itemize}
